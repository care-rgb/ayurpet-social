# AyurPet social standard (v1, 5 Nov 2026)

Karan, 5 Nov 2026: "It is not looking premium. It is not looking branded. It is looking like a college student is running
it." Grid audit the same day: 12 posts, seven visual languages (mustard serif collab covers, AI 3D dogs, a glowing brain
effect, webcam faces with burned-in subtitles, cartoon Diwali posters, template carousels, a contest poster). Every post
from now on follows this page. Anything that breaks it does not post.

The feeling to aim for: a family that loves its dogs, at home, in good light. Calm, warm, real. Not a startup and not a
festival poster. References for taste: Native Pet, Wild One, Fable Pets, The Farmer's Dog.

## One look
- **Real photography only.** Real dogs, real homes, natural window light, warm and slightly muted. India homes and
  Indian people on @ayurpetofficial; Western homes on @ayurpet_global.
  - Never: AI 3D renders, glow or neon effects, cartoons or illustrations, AI dogs.
- **Three colours.** AyurPet green, warm cream and one marigold accent. Nothing else on the graphics.
- **One typeface family.** The geometric sans from the store (lowercase headlines), at one size for headlines and one
  for body. No serif, no italics, no script fonts, no outlined or glowing text.
- **Words on images.** The cover carries 3 to 6 words, always in the same place (the lower third, on a cream band or
  straight on the photo). Slides carry 10 to 30 words. No subtitles burned into a cover.

## Grid
- Before scheduling, look at the 9-tile grid with the new post in it. It must read as one brand from across the room.
- Alternate reel, carousel, reel. Never two talking-head covers side by side.
- Every reel gets a designed cover that follows these rules. Never a raw frame of someone mid-word.
- Amanda Ree collab reels keep the Sama Dog cover only on @ayurpet_global, at most one in three tiles.

## Content mix per page per week
About 5 reels and 2 carousels:
- vet mistakes / "stop doing this" (one symptom)
- dog POV with a real dog and a human-sounding voice
- Amanda's emotional or "your dog" moments (global, 2 a week)
- community: contests, followers' dogs with our commentary
- Product posts: at most 1 in 5, never a "Shop now" card on a reach post.

## Reels
- **Hook:** within 2 seconds, the subject moving plus a line of 8 words or fewer.
- **Shape:** one idea, 30 to 60 s, ending on a loop.
- **Footage:** one hero dog throughout, real footage. Sound attached.
- **Captions:** one style, 3 to 6 words, never on a face.

## Captions
- At most 60 words: the hook line, one or two sentences of value, then one ask ("send this to...", "save this for...").
- At most 5 hashtags.
- A care disclaimer only where a health claim needs it, in one short line.

## Before anything posts
- **Automatic checks:** specs, faces, captions, sound, and the lip-sync guard for Amanda reels.
- **Human check:** a teammate (Kainaat) watches every reel with sound and looks at the 9-tile grid.
- **Karan's OK:** the calendar entry turns READY only after it.
