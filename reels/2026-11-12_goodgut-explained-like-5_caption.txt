How to fix your dog's gut, explained like you're five 🐶

Eating grass, licking paws, loose stools. Most of the time it isn't a skin problem. It starts in the gut.

A happy gut needs three things:
1. Break food down (digestive enzymes)
2. Calm the gut (triphala, milk thistle, bhumi amla)
3. Feed the good bacteria (prebiotics from banana, pumpkin, sweet potato and chicory root)

GoodGut+ does all three. A few drops a day, mixed into his food, and dogs love the taste.
